About

It started with a piano.

I've played since first grade, and at some point that turned into playing for residents at Sorento Rosa and Gordon Lane. It began as a favor and became a weekly habit I didn't want to give up. Between songs I'd read the newspaper aloud, help with exercises, or just sit with someone who wanted company.

Watching memory and mood shift week to week, sometimes hour to hour, is what pulled me toward psychiatry rather than away from geriatrics generally. The residents who were hardest to reach were the ones I thought about most, and I wanted to understand why. Geriatric psychiatry sits right at that intersection of aging, cognition, and mental health, and it's where I'd like to end up. Internal medicine is my secondary interest.

I'm still early in this, a first-year figuring out what the path actually looks like. But I've tried to build real exposure into every year: clinical certifications, a hospital internship, a multi-year research project, and a nonprofit built around the same population I hope to eventually treat.
