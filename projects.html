Research

AI Wildfire DetectionKSEA Drone Research

2023 to 2025

A three-year research project I led as the first high school participant at the UKC conference. Each year built on the last, starting with conceptual drone models for public alerts in remote areas and pollution tracking, then moving into wildfire detection using image classification algorithms trained on synthetic datasets and validated with real drone trials. The next phase is real-time implementation.

97% detection accuracy. Presented in Dallas, San Francisco, and Atlanta.

Pediatric Cardiac Detection PatchROP Medical Innovations

2024

A team prototype concept for proactive detection of cardiac events in children, using an enzyme-level detection patch. I led the poster presentation at the Medical Innovations Competition. The course pushed us through the full arc of clinical design, from identifying an unmet need to defending a prototype.

Software

CPR & Narcan Education AppFlutter, Dart

2023

An educational app built to teach CPR and Narcan administration, designed around rising opioid overdose cases. I focused on visual graphics that would help retention under stress, since the moment someone needs this information is the worst possible moment to be reading dense text.

Best 10th Grade App, Congressional App Challenge, California 40th District
